### Economy and equipment › Pet levels, weight and earnings

A pet card shows several traits. **Rarity** sets its baseline and level ceiling, **appearance** is Normal/Gold/Diamond/Rainbow, and **Volted** can appear beside any appearance. Pets also have individual size and weight, age, feeding XP and paid stat points. The atlas lists standard size-1, weight-roll-1 specimens with zero age, XP and upgrades.

| Rarity | Max level | Total XP | XP per level | Speed cap | Size cap | Jump / Earn cap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Common | 10  | 4,500 | 500 | 30  | 30  | 30  |
| Uncommon | 20  | 9,500 | 500 | 60  | 60  | 60  |
| Rare | 40  | 20,000 | 512.82 | 60  | 100 | 120 |
| Epic | 60  | 40,000 | 677.97 | 60  | 100 | 180 |
| Legendary | 80  | 70,000 | 886.08 | 60  | 100 | 240 |
| Mystic | 100 | 100,000 | 1,010.1 | 60  | 100 | 300 |

| Purchased stat | Per point | Limits and use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Speed | +0.5% to the speed multiplier | Up to 60 points, subject to level. A full Common can buy 30 points. Very high final speeds taper toward the 300 stud/s cap. |
| Jump | +0.1% to mount JumpPower | Base JumpPower 85. Level cap applies. |
| Size | +1 percentage point of growth | Up to 100 points. Visual growth adds size in five 10% steps, capped at 1.5× the species scale in the shared growth formula. |
| Earn | +0.3% to the base income multiplier | Level cap applies. Earn and Speed are the most expensive tracks for a given pet. |

XP needed per level is the rarity's total XP divided by **max level − 1**. Cash points cost more as you add points to a track. For the next point the code starts from the greater of 100 Cash or 25.5 seconds of that pet's base income, then applies track price weight, prior points and the habitat price scale. Track weights are Speed 5, Jump 1, Size 2 and Earn 5. The shop rounds the result. Food and upgrades have independent jobs.

**Age and size.** Placed and ridden pets age during active play. Placed pets also age offline. Every 600 seconds of age shows as one animal year, up to 2,000. Natural growth updates in 10-year steps. Growth combines age, the Size track and any legacy growth already recorded in the save. Effective weight scales with the cube of size. Higher relative weight can help an owned mount’s speed. A heavier-than-normal sleeping catch can slow the person carrying it.

**Passive Cash.** An awake placed pet earns its base rate × 1.2 balance boost × rebirth multiplier × (1 + Friend Boost) × permanent multipliers, including the Index boost. An active party can double total ranch payout. A recovering placed pet earns half. A stored pet and a pet you are riding earn no passive Cash from that slot. On a fresh profile, a just-awake size-1 Grassland Common with no other boosts illustrates the baseline at **$6/s**. Catching it also builds Index completion, so your actual HUD may be slightly higher.

**Selling.** A standard size-1, weight-roll-1 Grassland Common has a **$600** sale baseline. The price follows the species income ladder, 120 seconds of baseline, individual capture size, initial weight roll and appearance/mutation income factors. Age, upgrades, personal wealth, rebirth, friend and perk multipliers do not raise it. A Rare-or-higher pet, a pet with special looks or mutation, or an individual priced at $100,000 or more asks for confirmation.

### Economy and equipment › Use a radar and manage Wood

Hold a radar from Bag or the hotbar and click or tap the world. For Common through Legendary, choose a biome with that rarity currently available. A **Mystic Radar** searches across biomes. Follow the arrow and distance indicator toward the target. A radar gives you a location, while your character still needs the correct gate unlocked to travel there.

-   Only one radar can run. Turn the current one off before selecting a different rarity.
-   A scan starts with **5 minutes**. If you pick up the marked pet, its tracking clock pauses during the carry.
-   The matching radar is spent when an eligible catch is delivered. If another player catches the target, the radar shuts off and stays in your inventory. A timeout with no delivery also keeps it.
-   A target can disappear or move. If there's no eligible target, check the biome selection and the rare-animal status later. The hidden Unlimited Radar entry is a legacy perk.
