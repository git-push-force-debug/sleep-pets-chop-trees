### Frequently asked questions › Getting started and capture FAQ

### Where do I get my starter money?

Open **Rewards → Missions** and claim both welcome cards. The gifts are $10,000 and 100 Wood. The Dart Pistol costs $5,000 and the Baseball Bat costs 20 Wood during the guided route. Skipping supplies the eligible welcome gifts and missing starter tools.

### What do I do first after the tutorial?

Fill your pen with pets you can bring back reliably. Claim available free rewards, check which pet Rebirth needs, and build up a Wood reserve with a starter melee tool.

### Why are my darts missing?

Keep the animal in the crosshair, close the gap if it is out of range, and move clear of rocks, tree trunks and terrain. You and the target must be in the target's home biome. Lower ground can also help if the cliff-guard message appears.

### Is there an Auto Aim button?

The current HUD removed Auto Aim and its T shortcut. Aim through the center crosshair and scope manually.

### How do I make the gun stronger?

Open **Upgrade → Guns**, choose a gun you own, and buy its Sleep DMG points. Upgrades belong to that gun. Buy Ammo, Reload or Scope when those are the limits you keep running into.

### Why does my gun put one Fox to sleep faster than another?

The atlas shows a size-1 Normal baseline. A bigger body, special appearance or a Volted mutation can increase Sleep HP. Upgrades on the gun change how much each landed hit contributes.

### Why can't I pick up a sleeping animal?

Get closer and wait for the pickup prompt. Another player's claim starts with a five-second window. An animal can wake, be picked up by someone else, or be out of reach from where you stand.

### Can I pick up from a mount?

Yes, if you are close enough for the pickup prompt. The mounting case uses the mount's size to adjust the reach, and the normal capture checks still apply.

### Where does the carry timer stop?

At the paved hub boundary. Walk further into your own enclosure to finish claiming the pet. Watch for the **Safe!** and then the capture notifications.

### Can I drop it to help a friend?

Dropping a wild sleeper puts it on a pickup timer. Claim protection and ownership rules still apply. A knocked-loose carry has no normal protection. The game has no regular direct pet-trading menu in the reviewed player flow.

### Can someone steal a pet from my pen?

The theft mechanic concerns a sleeping wild catch being carried in the wild. An already-owned enclosure pet is protected.

### What if I leave while holding a catch?

An undelivered wild pet is lost when you leave. Carry it into the enclosure before quitting if you want to keep it.

### Why is my pet doing nothing in the Bag?

Stored pets are safe inventory. Earnings require a placed pet. **Ranch → Place Best** can bring your best earners into the pen.

### Frequently asked questions › Ranch, riding and logging FAQ

### Why is a new pet earning half?

It is recovering from the wild capture. Keep it placed and let it wake. Night speeds recovery, and the permanent perk stacks with night.

### Can feeding wake a pet?

Food adds XP. The recovery timer, night bonus, recovery perk and Wake Now use a separate system.

### Can a Common pet keep leveling forever?

Common pets top out at level 10 and 4,500 XP. Mystic pets top out at level 100 and 100,000 XP. See all six ceilings in [Pet levels](#pet-math).

### Why can I see an upgrade but can't buy it?

Check the wallet, level and track cap. Animal and gun tracks use Cash. Melee tracks use Wood. Feeding increases pet XP and opens additional room for stat points.

### Where did my pet go after riding?

It becomes a held animal when you dismount. Click or tap inside your own enclosure to place it, or use its card where the place action is shown.

### Can every pet fly?

Four can fly in this roster. They are Griffin, Sand Dragon, Dragon and Violet Wyvern. The other two Mystics, Cerberus and Yeti, stay on the ground.

### Why can't I dismount right now?

The driver needs to be grounded. Descend or use **Land**, then select **Dismount**. A passenger can leave using their passenger action or Jump.

### Why does my mount turn transparent when I aim?

The current HUD automatically fades a mount if its body blocks your crosshair in gun mode. Other players still see the mount. Scoping can hide the rider and mount on your own screen.

### Can two people ride one pet?

Yes. One drives and one uses **Ride along**. Board while the mount is grounded and the rear seat is free.

### Do trees use up my bat?

Melee tools are owned equipment. Use the same one repeatedly. Higher tiers and Tree DMG upgrades make later trees easier to fell.

### Why isn't Auto Chop doing anything?

Take out your melee tool, switch Auto Chop on, face an eligible tree and get close enough. Wait if the target is still a stump. Menus, being down and carrying a wild pet can prevent a swing.

### Does Auto Chop collect every tree in the biome?

It checks for a reachable trunk ahead. Your tool's sweep and cleave determine what the actual swing can hit. Move manually to new spots.

### Which trees are worth the most Wood?

Golden trees give five times their normal base amount. Reference base yield rises from 2 Wood in the Grassland to 150 in Enchanted Grove before boosters. Taller individuals can pay more.

### Do melee hits take a player's health?

The system is built around knockback and ragdolls. A player can lose a carried sleeper outside the safe zone if they get hit.

### Frequently asked questions › Rebirth, wildlife and rewards FAQ

### Do I lose my animals when I rebirth?

The currency route resets Cash and pays the Wood cost. The required pet and the rest of your collection stay. The Robux skip keeps your balance too.

### Does my next required animal change if I rejoin?

The requirement is saved for that stage. Look at **Rebirth**. Any owned specimen of the requested species, placed or stored, qualifies.

### Why won't this gate open?

Check its rebirth number. The order is Grassland, Savanna, Snowland, Desert, Prehistoric, Enchanted Grove. The final enclosure requires one more stage after Enchanted opens.

### Does a stronger mount let me skip a gate?

The same gate restrictions apply to ground movement and flying. Rebirth to gain access.

### Where is the Legendary or Mystic that just spawned?

The ordinary announcement leaves out its biome. Check the relevant radar and the HUD. Common through Legendary scans ask for a biome. Mystic searches all biomes.

### Why did my radar turn off and stay in Bag?

A timeout or another player's capture can end the scan without spending it. Successful delivery of a matching tracked catch spends the radar.

### Why didn't I see a rare spawn as soon as the timer hit zero?

Scheduling is per server. Finding valid spawn ground can fail, and a Mystic already alive prevents another scheduled Mystic. The timer also rotates between rarity labels.

### Can Volted and Rainbow appear together?

Yes. Rainbow is an appearance and Volted is a mutation. A wild pet can have both. See [Spawns and weather](#wildlife) for the separate rolls.

### Do the Index labels show exact wild odds?

The Index's “1 in N” values are flavor. Base wildlife uses species selection weights and caps, scheduled rarities use timers, and paid crates list probabilities for each outcome.

### Can I claim the next daily reward if I missed yesterday?

Yes. The next claim advances one slot after UTC midnight. Skipping a day does not reset the calendar.

### Why does another player have different daily missions?

The game picks three from the same pool for each player and UTC day. Quests are persistent and have the same thresholds.

### Frequently asked questions › Shopping and troubleshooting FAQ

### Where are the older gift bundles, pet displays and global Dart Levels?

The current Shop hides legacy Starter, Rider, Pro, Unlimited Radar and the old dart pack. Current premium pets come through crates and ordinary reward/capture paths. Dart stats now belong to the individual gun. Older snapshots can show different menus.

### Why does a Robux button say Coming soon?

The server only enables a configured prompt when Roblox returns an expected product or pass price. A missing, unavailable or mismatched product can leave the button disabled.

### Why are crates unavailable for me?

The game checks Roblox's eligibility result for paid random items. If eligibility cannot be established, the purchase flow remains unavailable.

### Can I get pets without Robux?

Yes. Capture wild animals and claim eligible daily, invite and quest rewards. Some later prizes and premium options are simply additional routes.

### When does a gift arrive?

A player in your server may receive it after the purchase clears. Gifts to friends elsewhere can be queued. Keep the purchase details if delivery fails so the owner can trace the grant.

### What if I already own a perk somebody sends?

The purchase flow tries to detect it before charging. A duplicate that arrives afterward can be credited as Cash according to the store's fallback logic.

### Why wasn't I paid offline?

Check that animals were placed and you were away for at least 30 seconds. The **Welcome back!** card appears with a positive claim. A pet left stored after a ride is outside the placed roster.

### How do I quiet the experience?

Use **Settings** for Music, Sound effects, Full effects, Camera shake, Animal name tags and Tree hit flash. Roblox's Reduce Motion option also reduces decorative animation. The settings described here are wired in the current source.

### Why is the loading bar near 99%?

The client may still be waiting for the world, profile, original assets, terrain near spawn, or the character. The loading-screen **Skip** dismisses the visual overlay while the underlying readiness checks continue. If the loader reports a failure, use its retry action if shown or rejoin. Loading-screen Skip and tutorial Skip are separate buttons.

### Where do I report a bug?

Use the hub's **Feedback** stand. Include the biome, what you pressed, what happened and the exact message if there was one. Use a short note and leave out payment credentials or personal contact details.
