### Player guide › Start here

Sleep Pets & Chop Trees is a ranch and collection game. Put wild animals to sleep with dart guns, carry them across the brick edge into the hub, and settle them in your enclosure. Placed pets earn Cash on their own. Cut trees with melee tools for Wood, then use both currencies and the required pet to rebirth and open more of the map.

> **Your first session.** Claim both welcome gifts in Rewards. Buy the Dart Pistol for **$5,000** and, when the tutorial sends you back, the Baseball Bat for **20 Wood**. Make your first catch in the Grassland close to the brick path. Keep the first pets in your pen to earn while you explore.

| What you want to do | Where to go |
| --- | --- |
| Catch your first pet | [The tutorial](#tutorial) and [capture walkthrough](#capture) |
| Buy or equip a weapon | **Weapon Store** at the hub. **Upgrade** has per-weapon upgrades. |
| Find a pet you own | **Ranch** for placed pets, **Bag** for stored items, **Upgrade → Animals** for all owned pets. |
| Make more Cash | Fill the pen, place your best earners, claim [Rewards](#rewards), and [rebirth](#rebirth). |
| Make more Wood | Equip a melee tool and [chop trees](#chop), including golden trees. |
| Find an animal or compare pets | [Biome atlas](#world) and [species tables](#grassland). |
| Find a quick answer | [Player FAQ](#faq) and [troubleshooting](#troubleshooting). |

### Player guide › First ranch, step by step

The tutorial uses white arrows on the ground, a pointing finger on menus, and a guide dialog. Close a menu with its red **X** if the guide tells you to switch. Follow its prompts in order on your first visit.

| Step | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| 1\. Claim your welcome gifts | Press **Next** on the greeting. Open **Rewards → Missions**. Press **Claim** for **$10,000**, then press the separate **Claim** for **100 Wood**. |
| 2\. Buy the Dart Pistol | Follow the arrows to the **Weapon Store**. Press **E**, use the gamepad prompt, or tap. Open **Guns** and buy the **Dart Pistol** for **$5,000**. |
| 3\. Go to the Grassland | Cross the end of the brick apron. The Grassland starts immediately beyond it. |
| 4\. Take out the gun | Press **2** or tap the gun slot. The crosshair appears. |
| 5\. Put an animal to sleep | Aim the crosshair at a nearby Common pet and shoot until its sleep progress reaches 100%. A normal-sized Deer, Fox, or Boar needs three clean Dart Pistol hits at base power. |
| 6\. Pick it up | Approach the sleeping pet and press **E** or tap **Pick Up**. |
| 7\. Bring it home | Head straight back across the brick edge, then walk into **your own** fenced enclosure. Wait for the capture notification. |
| 8\. Improve the gun | Open **Upgrade → Guns → Dart Pistol** and buy **+1 Sleep DMG**. The first point costs **$540**. |
| 9\. Buy the bat | Return to the **Weapon Store → Melee** and buy the **Baseball Bat** for **20 Wood**. |
| 10\. Chop a Grassland tree | Press **1** or tap the melee slot. Face a trunk outside the hub and swing until the tree falls. |
| 11\. Buy food | Go back to **Food Market**. Open its prompt, select **Cabbage**, then use the green **$1,000** price button. |
| 12\. Feed your pet | Open **Upgrade → Animals → your animal → Feed** and give it one serving. |
| 13\. Buy a pet stat | Return to the animal card and use **+1** on a stat. Finishing the first run pays a one-time **$1,000** tutorial reward. |

**Skip and replay.** Skipping delivers any eligible welcome gifts and puts missing starter equipment in inventory. The completion bonus comes from finishing the tutorial. To review the instructions, use **Settings → Replay** after the tutorial has ended. A replay begins at the weapon step and pays no extra tutorial rewards. First-time tutorial players get protection from direct wild attacks and have storm visuals suppressed. Nearby wild animals are slowed for the first-time tutorial.

### Player guide › Find, aim and put a pet to sleep

1.  **Pick a target you can bring home.** Early on, work near the Grassland entrance. The biome badge tells you where you are. A target has to be in its home biome, and you need to be inside that same biome when you fire.
2.  **Use an open line of sight.** Equip the dart gun and move the center crosshair onto the animal. The reticle and hit feedback help confirm aim. Terrain, rocks, tree trunks and buildings can stop the dart. The high boundary cliffs are also blocked for capture. Scoped view moves to eye height and hides your character and mount locally.
3.  **Build up sleep progress.** Each hit adds that gun's Sleep DMG to your own progress on the target. Another player's hits build their own bar. The first player to reach 100% puts the pet to sleep. Melee flings a wild animal instead of adding sleep progress. A hit on a conscious animal often makes it flee or charge.
4.  **Reload when needed.** Ammo is a magazine count. Reload a partial magazine with the reload input, or let an empty one reload automatically. The gun's tag or upgrade card shows magazine size, reload, rate, range and Sleep DMG. The current gun uses manual aim.

**When a pet lies down, move in.** Wild sleep usually lasts **45 seconds**. The player who put it to sleep has the first **5 seconds** of pickup protection. After that, the first eligible player to pick it up takes it. The reach starts from a 14-stud setting and is adjusted for animal size and mounting. Fly close enough to get the prompt. A flying pet falls or glides toward the ground when sedated.

**Animals react differently.** Timid and alert wildlife tend to run. Aggressive animals can retaliate after a hit. Aggressive Epic and higher animals can charge on approach. Mystic flyers can dive. At night, wildlife detection distance is 75% of its daytime distance. Ordinary player walking speed is 26 studs per second, so use a clear approach and consider a mount for longer chases.

[section:carry] [chapter:player-guide] [images:]
### Player guide › Carry the catch home

When you pick up a sleeping wild pet, it rides overhead and the **Bring it home** bar starts. The game gives the carried pet a travel timer based on your distance to your pen, the biome, its rarity and appearance, and how much it slows you down. A heavy roll relative to the pet’s species can slow you more. For the Grassland the allowance is tuned around walking. Later biomes use a pace based on a normal Rare mount from the previous biome.

-   **Cross the brick edge first.** As soon as your delivery is safely across the Grassland-to-hub line, the carry clock stops. The **Safe! Put it in your enclosure** message tells you that you can finish the walk through the square.
-   **Finish inside your fence.** Step into your own enclosure or use **Drop** over it to deliver. An open pen slot places the pet. If the pen is full, delivery stores it, and it can appear in a hotbar slot if there is room. If both are full, make a space before trying to finish.
-   **Keep moving through the wild.** A rival can knock a carried pet out of your hands before you reach safety. A stolen drop has no pickup protection. A player who successfully delivers somebody else's knocked-loose pet can complete the steal mission. Pets in the ranch are protected from theft.
-   **A manual drop starts a ground timer.** A pet you drop outside your pen lands on the ground and stays asleep for at least 15 seconds, or for the remaining carry time if that is longer. It can be picked up again. If it wakes, it runs toward its habitat. Flying drops use the ground below. Leaving the server with an unfinished carry loses it.

**What changes at dusk?** Night helps recovery after delivery and makes wildlife slower to notice you. The carry timer counts down at the same speed day and night.

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Protected** | Wait for the short protection window to end if another player sedated it. |
| **Get closer** | Close the gap to the pet or get near it on your mount. |
| **Hurry! It's waking up!** | Head straight for the brick edge. The red bar indicates the final quarter. |
| **Safe!** | Walk into your enclosure and look for the capture notification. |
| **Ranch and bag are full!** | Free a storage or pen slot. Keep track of any pet you are still carrying. |
| **It woke up and ran away!** | A lost catch has to be sedated and picked up again. It bolts during its first few seconds. |

[section:ranch] [chapter:player-guide] [images:r8-feed-prompt,gate3-play-client]
### Player guide › Run your ranch and train pets

You get an enclosure when your profile loads. The place has six authored plots around the hub. A new ranch has **6 placed slots** and room for **60 stored animals** under normal capture and storage rules. Each rebirth adds one pen slot, up to **12**. Some reward and crate grants can land in storage beyond the normal capture cap.

| Action | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| See your pen | Open **Ranch**. Its counter shows placed animals against capacity. |
| Inspect a pet | Open its card from **Upgrade → Animals**, the Ranch list or the Bag. Check its rarity, traits, recovery, earnings, size, weight, age, XP and upgrades. |
| Place a stored pet | Use **Place** on its card where offered, or hold it and click/tap an open spot inside your fence. |
| Move a placed pet | Use the placement action from its ranch card. Pick a floor spot with room inside your own fence. |
| Store a pet | Use the pet card's **Unequip** action to return a placed pet to Bag. |
| Fill with top earners | **Place Best** sorts pets by base income and fills the enclosure. The currently ridden pet is left alone. |
| Feed | Use **Feed** on the pet card, or hold food and approach or tap the pet. A feeding adds the food's XP. |
| Improve a stat | On the pet card, spend Cash with **+1** or **MAX** where shown. **MAX** stops at the cap or when Cash runs out. |
| Decorate | Open **Ranch → Decorate**. Buy props with Wood and choose one of the six fixed spots. An owned prop can be reused or removed. |
| Sell | Visit **Sell Stand**. Select individual pets or a batch, review the value, and confirm. The sale is permanent. |

**Recovery.** A newly captured pet arrives recovering. It earns half its awake rate while placed and cannot be ridden until awake. Recovery work spans **2 minutes** for a Grassland Common to **90 minutes** for an Enchanted Mystic at daytime base speed. Night runs remaining recovery at **10×** and the permanent recovery perk adds **4×**, for **40×** together. The displayed remaining time changes when the rate changes. A reward or crate pet is granted ready to use. **Wake Now** is a paid shortcut for an owned pet still recovering.

**Training.** Food gives XP. XP opens room for separately purchased Cash stat upgrades. At level 1 you can already buy three points per stat. Each level adds room for three more, subject to each stat's cap. Read [Pet levels and earnings](#pet-math) for the rarity ceilings and exact stat effects.

### Player guide › Ride, fly and take a passenger

**Ride an awake pet you own.** Walk up to it in the ranch and use **Ride**, or hold it from Bag and click or tap the **Ride** button. Mounting a placed pet moves it out of its earning slot and into the stored/riding state. When you dismount it comes back to your hands. Place it in the enclosure again to resume passive income from that pet.

**On the ground.** Move with the normal controls. Tap Jump for a hop. Ground speed depends on biome, rarity, appearance, mutation, size, weight and Speed upgrades. Jump upgrades affect the jump. Dismount on the ground with the visible button, or the controller **B** binding. **E** is the ranch pet prompt for getting on.

**In the air.** Griffin, Sand Dragon, Dragon and Violet Wyvern can fly. Hold Jump for roughly half a second to take off, or use the **Fly** button on touch. Keep Jump held to climb, hold **Q** or the descent control to go down, or select **Land**. The flight ceiling is about **250 studs of world height**. Biome gates and world edges still apply. You can aim and fire a dart gun in flight when the normal capture checks pass. Land before a driver dismounts. If the animal you are riding covers the crosshair in unscoped gun mode, it fades locally. Scoping can hide the mount and rider on your screen.

**Ride along.** Approach another player's grounded mount and use its **Ride along** prompt. It has one passenger seat behind the driver. You need free hands for a carried catch and the seat must be free. The driver steers. A passenger can leave with the passenger action or Jump even during flight and is placed down toward the ground. A hit on a mount can move both riders. A carried pet and a flying mount need extra space near the ground, gates and trees.

**Mount action.** **R** or **Y** with the gun away plays the mount's attack animation. The riding path triggers an animation. Wild charge knockback and equipped melee strikes have their own combat rules.

[section:chop] [chapter:player-guide] [images:]
### Player guide › Chop trees and handle knockback

Equip the **Baseball Bat** or another melee weapon. Walk up to a trunk, face it and click or tap to swing. Watch the chips and progress. When it falls, Wood is credited to your wallet and the trunks fly toward your HUD. The starter bat has 1 base Tree DMG, a 0.5-second swing and one-tree cleave. A reference Grassland tree needs six ordinary starter hits. Critical hits and tree size change that count.

| Logging rule | Current build |
| --- | --- |
| Auto Chop | Equip melee and click the **Auto Chop** icon over Rebirth and Upgrade. The green arrow means on. It swings at the weapon's pace when a tree is in reach in front of you. It does not walk you to the next tree. |
| Golden tree | 3% roll on setup and regrowth. Look for the golden sparkle. It pays 5× the tree's usual Wood before your modifiers. |
| Critical hit | 15% chance per tree hit. A critical hit does 2× tree damage. |
| Regrowth / healing | A fallen tree regrows after about 7 seconds. Unfinished chop damage heals after 20 seconds unattended. A regrowing trunk can remain walk-through until the space clears. |
| Shared tree | Players who dealt damage share its Wood by contribution, with a minimum one Wood for eligible contributors. |
| Later weapons | More Tree DMG and more cleave at higher tiers. The first three tools have blade hits only. Starting at Cenarius, a growing crescent can reach trees and other valid targets. |
| Wood boosters | Earn upgrades, rebirths and the 2× Wood perk stack on tree payout. Fractional bonus Wood is rounded by chance across hits. |

**Player versus player.** Outside the ranch safe area, a melee blow can fling and briefly ragdoll a player or mount. It does not take health as conventional weapon damage. Ragdoll duration is roughly **1.6–3 seconds**. After a player's hit ends, there is a **3-second** guard against the next player hit. Body weight affects knockback. If you're delivering a catch, the fastest protection is to cross back into the ranch square. Swinging and tree chopping still work in the safe area.

**Spend Wood on purpose.** Wood buys melee weapons, their four stat tracks, decorations and the Wood part of rebirth. The Shop also sells Wood packs. A larger or later-biome tree takes more work and grants more Wood, so match your tool to the habitat. [Compare the full melee catalog](#melee).

[section:rebirth] [chapter:player-guide] [images:]
### Player guide › Unlock the six ranch tiers

Open **Rebirth** to see the exact species your profile needs. It is one Common, Uncommon or Rare from the biome you are finishing. A pet in the Bag also counts. The requirement is saved for that stage. Keep the pet until you rebirth. The progress bar uses the lower of your Cash and Wood progress, and the ready badge also checks the animal.

| Stage | Cash needed | Wood | Pet from | Enclosure | Opens | Slots | Cash / Wood |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1   | $280,000 | 250 | Grassland | Meadow | Savanna | 7   | ×2 / ×1.5 |
| 2   | $4,000,000 | 800 | Savanna | Safari | Snowland | 8   | ×4 / ×2.25 |
| 3   | $20,000,000 | 5,000 | Snowland | Glacier | Desert | 9   | ×8 / ×3.375 |
| 4   | $80,000,000 | 20,000 | Desert | Oasis | Prehistoric | 10  | ×16 / ×5.0625 |
| 5   | $800,000,000 | 100,000 | Prehistoric | Jurassic | Enchanted Grove | 11  | ×32 / ×7.5938 |
| 6   | $16,000,000,000 | 500,000 | Enchanted Grove | Crystal | Final tier | 12  | ×64 / ×11.3906 |

A currency rebirth resets your Cash balance to **0**, deducts the listed Wood and keeps your pets, tools, upgrades, Index and other inventory. The required animal also stays in your collection. A **199 Robux** skip can advance one stage and keeps your balances and collection. Each stage adds a pen slot and a new enclosure style. Each doubles passive Cash and multiplies tree Wood by **1.5×** relative to the previous stage.

### A practical progression route

1.  Fill the initial six slots with catches near the Grassland entrance. Claim the daily calendar and any ready missions or Index rewards.
2.  Keep your required species, buy the melee tool you need for reliable logging and get at least one pet recovered for riding. Spend on upgrades before a currency rebirth if the upgrade fits your next habitat and you already have enough to meet the rebirth threshold afterward.
3.  Enter the newly opened biome. Replace weak earners gradually and keep a fast mount available for the return trip. Check **Rebirth** as soon as you open the next stage so you know which species to keep.
4.  At stage six the **Crystal** enclosure, 12 slots and full multipliers are the final goals. Enchanted Grove itself opens one rebirth earlier.
