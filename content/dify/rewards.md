The ready badge counts available daily, mission and quest claims. **Claim All** collects the currently eligible daily reward, missions and unlocked quest tiers in one action. ### Rewards and Shop › Index, invites and codes

### Animal Index

Encountered pets can be marked seen. Catch or receive a species to count it as owned in the collection record. The Index remembers discoveries after a sale. It shows Normal, Gold, Diamond and Rainbow. The Normal count advances with the first capture of that species in any appearance, while the color categories need their own recorded variant. Volted is tracked separately.

| Index tab | Permanent Cash boost for each species recorded in that tab | At 72 entries |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Normal | +0.5% | +36% |
| Gold | +1% | +72% |
| Diamond | +2% | +144% |
| Rainbow | +4% | +288% |

These contributions add to a theoretical maximum **+540%**, equivalent to **6.4×** before the other multipliers. The Index's dramatic “1 in N” labels are flavor text. The wild refill uses weights and schedules, and paid crates show their own exact odds. Every four unique species opens a Cash claim, up to **18 milestones**. [See all milestone floors](#index-reference).

### Friends and invitations

Every verified Roblox friend currently in the same server adds **+25%** to passive Cash, counted up to ten friends. Their contribution ends when they leave. The six authored plots make the theoretical cap higher than the number of friends that can ordinarily share a six-player ranch server. Separately, a distinct friend whose Roblox join credits your invite advances the Invite boxes. The four claim thresholds are **1 → Epic, 2 → Epic, 3 → Legendary, 4 → Legendary**. Invite claims and Friend Boost are counted independently.

### Redeem codes

### Rewards and Shop › Gifts and game passes

Choose an eligible Cash pack, Wood pack or permanent perk in **Send a Gift**. Select a player currently in the server or a verified Roblox friend. A friend in another server or offline can receive eligible queued gifts later, provided that service is available. The recipient gets the configured pack amount. Crates, passes and personal offline claims are excluded from gifting. If a one-time perk arrives after the recipient already owns it, the grant can convert to Cash. Duplicate premium weapon deliveries from the Roblox Store page also use a Cash fallback.

**Gift check before you pay.** Check the recipient and the item in the gift picker. The server rejects self-gifts, hidden offers and already-owned one-time perks when recipient data is available. Delivery to someone outside the current server depends on friendship and the gift queue being reachable.

### Rewards and Shop › Every crate and exact odds

Open **Shop** and choose the crate. Buy **1, 3 or 10 rolls** where the offer is available. Each roll grants one pet, with the probabilities below. The Wild Crate mixes the habitats. Each regional crate takes all six listed outcomes from that biome. The ordinary Mystic chance is **1% per roll**. On the **100th roll since the last Mystic for the same crate**, the roll is guaranteed to be that crate's Mystic. Any Mystic from that crate resets that crate's counter. The tracker is saved across sessions and packs.

One outcome per roll. Pity can replace the ordinary roll with the Mystic in the 1% column. Prices above are in Robux.

**Delivery.** Crate pets are granted in their normal appearance with the built-in size roll. They go into a free pen slot or storage, and are ready to use without recovery. Paid random items are unavailable if the per-player policy check disallows them or cannot confirm eligibility. The pity counter applies only to that crate. A wild Legendary or Mystic spawn follows its own schedule.

### Offline earnings

When you return after at least 30 seconds away with placed animals, the game calculates the absence using up to **24 hours** at **50%** of the eligible ranch rate. The calculation ages those placed pets, uses their fully recovered payout rate and the profile's normal permanent multipliers, and omits Friend Boost. Only positive earnings create a claim. In **Welcome back!**, choose **Claim 1×** for free or the paid **Claim 2×**. The paid double path waits for the Roblox receipt. Keep earners placed before you leave.
