### This website › What this site is

This is the official help and guide site for the Roblox game Sleep Pets & Chop Trees. It has the full player guide (controls, capturing pets, the ranch, chopping trees, rebirths), the six biomes and all 72 animals, the gear catalog, rewards and crates, and a FAQ. Use the "Play on Roblox" button to open the game on Roblox.

=====

[section:site-chat] [chapter:site] [images:]
### This website › How the chat with Pip works

Pip is the site's helper bot. Pip answers only from the official game guide and shows the guide section it used as a source link, sometimes with a table, steps or a picture. If the guide doesn't cover your question, Pip offers to bring in a real person from the team. Pip cannot see your Roblox account, your purchases or your inventory.

=====

[section:site-person] [chapter:site] [images:]
### This website › Talking to a real person

Press "Talk to a person" at the top of the chat at any time. Add your Roblox username so the team can find you, and an email if you want a reply after you leave. Your chat with Pip is shared with the team, so you don't need to repeat yourself. A person from the team then replies in the same chat window. Replies can take a while when the team is offline; keep this browser open or come back later on the same device to see them.

=====

[section:site-ticket] [chapter:site] [images:]
### This website › Ticket numbers and closed chats

When a person joins, your chat gets a ticket number, for example Ticket #128. Mention it if you contact the team again. When the team marks your ticket as resolved, that chat closes and can't receive new messages. Press "Start new chat" if you need help with something else.

[section:site-safety] [chapter:site] [images:]
### This website › Staying safe

The team will never ask for your Roblox password, a verification code or payment details in the chat. Never share them with anyone. The website cannot give out free items, Robux or secret codes; working codes are listed in the Rewards section of the guide.

=====

[section:site-problems] [chapter:site] [images:]
### This website › Purchases, bugs and lost pets

Pip can't check purchases, refunds, bans or lost pets. For a missing game pass or Robux purchase, a bug, or a pet that disappeared, choose "Talk to a person" and describe what happened, including roughly when it happened. For quick in-game feedback you can also use the Feedback board in the Ranch Hub.
