# Art briefs — Sleep Pets & Chop Trees website

Owner: Claude (visual direction). Generation: Codex (image_gen). Claude reviews every output.

## Shared style (applies to every asset)

- **Look:** soft, stylized 3D "toy diorama" render. Chunky low-poly shapes with rounded edges, matte materials, gentle ambient occlusion, warm sunny light from the upper left. It should feel like the game's bright blocky world (grass terraces, brick plaza, wooden fences), but every asset is **original**.
- **Palette:**
  - meadow green `#3FA34D` / `#2E7D3A`
  - sky blue `#5BB8F0`
  - warm brick plaza `#D9917A`
  - fence wood `#8A5A3B`
  - cream `#FFF6E5`
  - golden accent `#FFC93C` with an orange base `#F08A24`
- **Mood:** cozy, playful, kid-friendly, sleepy. Sleeping animals curl up with small floating "Z z" shapes (drawn shapes, not typed text).
- **Never:** the Roblox logo or wordmark, Roblox avatars or any third-party character, brand or UI; photorealism; text anywhere except the logo; watermarks; gradients that look like stock AI art.

## Assets

Save each file to `apps/web/public/art/` with exactly this name.

1. **`logo.png`** — wordmark "Sleep Pets & Chop Trees" in chunky rounded 3D letters.
   - "Sleep Pets" in cream with a soft blue night glow; "& Chop Trees" in wood-grain brown. A small leaf sprouts from the "T", and three tiny "z" shapes float off the "S".
   - Transparent background. About 1600×700. Every letter must be legible and spelled exactly.
2. **`hero.webp`** — wide 2400×1100 diorama of the Ranch Hub seen from a slight elevation.
   - A brick plaza with a round fountain and four little market stalls (red, blue, gold, green awnings).
   - Fenced grass pens with sleeping animals: a deer, a fox and a curled-up cheetah, each with "Z z" shapes.
   - On the right, a stack of logs and a half-chopped tree with a cartoon axe resting against it. A griffin glides in the sky.
   - Grass terraces and cliffs fade into the distance under a clear sky.
   - **Keep the left 40% calm** (sky and soft grass) so a headline can sit there.
3. **`biome-grassland.webp`** (1200×800) — rolling meadow terraces and soil banks. A sleeping deer and fox, with a horse grazing. A bright daytime sky.
4. **`biome-savanna.webp`** (1200×800) — golden grass, sandstone rock arches and an acacia tree. A sleeping zebra and cheetah, with a giraffe in the background. Warm afternoon light.
5. **`biome-snowland.webp`** (1200×800) — snow shelves and pine trees. A sleeping red panda curled in snow, with a snow leopard on a ledge. Soft cool light.
6. **`biome-desert.webp`** (1200×800) — sandstone mesas and cacti. A sleeping fennec fox, with a camel resting. Golden-hour light.
7. **`biome-prehistoric.webp`** (1200×800) — giant ferns and a misty volcano in the distance. A sleeping baby triceratops, with a stegosaurus behind. Lush green with a warm haze.
8. **`biome-enchanted.webp`** (1200×800) — glowing violet-blue trees, crystal clusters and giant mushrooms. A sleeping glowing deer with faint sparkles. Dusk light in violet and teal.
9. **`mascot.png`** — "Pip", the website's helper. A round, sleepy baby fawn standing upright on its hind legs.
   - Pip wears a green ranch bandana, and a tiny leaf sits on one antler nub. Half-closed happy eyes, a small smile, holding a little wooden clipboard.
   - Front three-quarter view, full body, centered. Transparent background, 1024×1024. Friendly at 40 px as an avatar.
10. **`empty-chat.png`** — Pip asleep on a small log with a lantern beside it and "Z z" shapes floating up. Transparent background, 900×700.
11. **`og.png`** — 1200×630 social share card: a crop of the hero scene with the logo placed large on the left, readable as a small thumbnail. No other text.
