1SleepAim the dart gun and fill sleep progress to 100%.
2CarryPick it up and cross the brick edge before it wakes.
3RanchPlaced pets earn Cash. Feed them and buy stats.
4ChopSwing melee tools at trees for Wood. Golden trees pay 5×.
